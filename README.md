The novel is now assembled as a full-length draft of approximately **125,000 words** across a prologue, interlude, five story movements, twenty-four chapters, and an epilogue. The publication edition includes standard front matter, copyright language, dedication, content note, author’s note, and contents.

## Editorial and rights safeguards

The book is presented as **fiction**, not as a verified account of real government conduct, real criminal accusations, or real public figures. Real-world song lyrics are not reproduced. Music references remain descriptive or use original fictional fragments. The daughters’ privacy is treated as a central ethical boundary rather than as plot evidence.

The 2020 backstory is framed inside the novel as a difficult period that Skyler used for family responsibility, volunteering, school, work, and church. The story emphasizes gratitude for the opportunity to become useful and build a better life, without asserting unsupported claims about real judges, governments, or identifiable people.

## Remaining steps before Amazon publication

A professional copyedit should still review grammar, punctuation, repeated phrasing, dialogue formatting, chapter-heading consistency, and continuity. The author should confirm the final author name, copyright holder, dedication names, and privacy choices. The manuscript should then be converted into a reflowable Kindle file and a 6 × 9 inch paperback interior, followed by a proof copy. Cover artwork should be checked against the final trim size, bleed, spine width, and barcode requirements.

Amazon category names and keyword rules can change. Use the closest current dashboard categories and verify metadata at upload rather than treating the suggestions in `amazon_package.md` as guaranteed labels.

## Author’s note

The strongest positioning is a morally complex fictional thriller about technology, identity, power, accountability, and a father who chooses love and responsibility over revenge. The book should not be marketed as testimony or as a factual allegation against real people or institutions.
