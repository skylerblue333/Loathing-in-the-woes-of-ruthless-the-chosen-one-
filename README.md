# *Loathing in the Woes of Ruthlessness: The Chosen One*

## Publication Package

*A concise literary psychological techno-thriller by Skyler Blue.*

> When everyone controls the story, the only weapon left is the truth.

Skyler Blue is a gifted coder and father whose phone, reputation, relationships, and digital identity become contested territory. A fictional criminal influence network called the Pawns Network uses manipulated media, synthetic coincidences, political spectacle, and psychological pressure to turn his pain into a public weapon. To survive, Skyler must separate evidence from interpretation and decide whether he will become a weapon, a victim, a symbol, or a person capable of building something better.

## Fictionalization Statement

This is an original work of fiction. The organizations, agencies, technologies, criminal operations, media campaigns, and events depicted in the manuscript are fictionalized elements of a speculative thriller. The novel does not establish unsupported allegations about real governments, public figures, ethnic groups, or institutions as fact.
