### Book description

Skyler Blue built systems to protect vulnerable people.

Now his own life has become a system designed to destroy him.

After a devastating legal and family crisis, Skyler’s phone begins displaying impossible messages. Advertisements echo private memories. Songs seem to arrive with knowledge they should not possess. Political posts, church language, employment contacts, and strangers’ comments begin to rhyme around his life.

When an anonymous criminal network offers him twenty million dollars to become its digital operator, Skyler realizes the chaos may be more than surveillance. It may be an attempt to recruit him, frame him, or turn his pain into a public weapon.

The Pawns Network does not control everyone. It controls enough. It gives different people different stories until friends, employers, strangers, and institutions begin moving against the same target without knowing why.

With help from a compromised analyst known as Ghost, an investigative journalist, and an investigator who refuses to give him easy answers, Skyler creates the Truth Protocol: a record separating what he knows from what he believes and what he fears.

But evidence is not the same as justice. Winning the story may still cost him his humanity.

To protect his daughters, Skyler must decide what kind of chosen one he will become: a weapon, a victim, a symbol—or a man willing to build something better after the war is over.

### Short description

A father, a phone, and a hidden influence network collide in a psychological techno-thriller about surveillance, betrayal, digital identity, family, and the cost of becoming a symbol.

### Final publication checklist

Complete a professional copyedit for grammar, punctuation, repeated phrasing, scene transitions, dialogue formatting, chapter-heading consistency, pacing, and continuity. The family-edition pass has strengthened the dedication, “Before the Glitch” opening, 2020 second-chance arc, and keepsake material; the remaining copyedit should protect that voice rather than flatten it. Confirm every character name, relationship, age, object, location, and date against `novel_bible.md` and `loathing_chapter_plotline_timeline.md`. Confirm that the manuscript contains no private contact information, unsupported real-world allegations, identifiable details about uninvolved people, or reproduced song lyrics.

For Kindle, convert the Markdown master into a reflowable file with clean heading styles and a linked table of contents. For paperback, prepare a 6 × 9 inch interior with mirrored margins, embedded fonts, page numbers beginning after the front matter, and chapter openings appropriate to the selected layout. Order a proof copy before enabling distribution. Check the cover against trim, bleed, spine-width, and barcode requirements.

### Author bio draft

**Skyler Blue Spillers** is a software builder, founder, father, chess player, and writer whose work explores technology, identity, family, resilience, and the moral choices people make under pressure. *Loathing in the Woes of Ruthlessness: The Chosen One* blends psychological suspense with a deeply personal family story and a speculative look at how modern systems can shape what people believe.

### Positioning note

Present the book as an original fictional thriller. Do not describe it as testimony, a verified account of government wrongdoing, or an allegation against real public figures or groups. The strongest commercial position is the novel’s actual premise: a morally complex father caught inside a fictional influence operation that weaponizes technology, media, and uncertainty while he tries to protect the people he loves.
